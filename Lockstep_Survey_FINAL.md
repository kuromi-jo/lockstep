# Lockstep Survey: Final Questionnaire (Google Forms, one form)

**Course:** IT 021 – Usable Security · Group #1 · IT31S6
**Method:** Within-subjects, task-based comparison (Baseline → Lockstep) with a pre/post knowledge check
**Format:** One Google Form · closed-ended questions · about 12–15 minutes
**Form order:** Consent → About You → Pre-Check → Version A → Version B → Lockstep Features → Post-Check → Comparison

> ### What "Version A" and "Version B" mean
> **Version A = BASELINE ("Before").** The typical phone setup: choose a lock, a fixed 4-digit PIN, a plain "Skip" with no warning, an unexplained encryption switch that starts off, and no summary at the end.
> **Version B = LOCKSTEP ("After").** The redesign: plain-language explanations, a live protection meter, skip warnings, encryption "On, recommended", and a review screen with "Fix now".
> **A10–A20 and B10–B20 have the same wording.** Comparing them gives your Before/After results.

**Links**
- Version A: `https://<your-site>.netlify.app/baseline.html?study=1`
- Version B: `https://<your-site>.netlify.app/setup.html?study=1`

**Agreement scale (grids):** 1 Strongly disagree · 2 Disagree · 3 Neutral · 4 Agree · 5 Strongly agree

| "Other" option | Never use |

| Q1 branching | "I do not agree…" → Submit form |

1. **Deploy to Netlify, then replace [LINK A] and [LINK B]** with your live URLs (`baseline.html?study=1` and `setup.html?study=1`). Use the HTTPS links, because the breach check, real fingerprint/face option and Copy button need HTTPS.
